# Rule 10: Anti-AI Aesthetic & Bespoke Asset Standard

## 1. Prohibited Clichés
- **AI Purple/Black Themes:** Forbidden. Replace with deliberate, nature-inspired or enterprise domain palettes.
- **Primitive Emojis as Primary UI Icons:** Forbidden. Use high-quality vector SVGs (Lucide, Iconsax, Tabler) or generated domain SVGs in `assets/`.
- **AI Em-Dashes:** Forbidden. Strictly use the standard keyboard hyphen `-`.

---

## 2. Bespoke Brand Identity
- Every project must include a custom SVG brand logo (`assets/logo.svg`) and banner (`assets/banner.svg`).
- Maintain consistent branding across documentation, presentations, and dashboards.
