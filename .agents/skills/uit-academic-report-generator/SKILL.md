# UIT Academic Report Generator Skill

## Mission
Produces complete, publication-ready academic reports in Microsoft Word (`.docx`) format complying with **100% of the University of Information Technology (UIT - VNU-HCM)** thesis guidelines.

---

## 1. Mandatory Formatting Rules
- **Typography:** Times New Roman, 13pt for body text. Justified alignment.
- **Line Spacing:** 1.5 lines throughout all paragraphs.
- **Paragraph Spacing:** Before: 0pt, After: 6pt. First line indent: 1.0 cm.
- **Page Margins:**
  - Left: 3.0 cm (for thesis binding spine).
  - Right: 2.0 cm.
  - Top: 2.5 cm.
  - Bottom: 2.5 cm.

---
