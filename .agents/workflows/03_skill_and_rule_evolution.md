# SOP 03: Rule & Skill Evolution Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Formalizes developer corrections and structural enhancements into permanent system governance artifacts.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step SOP

```text
[Step 1: Ingest Developer Feedback]
  └── Identify gaps in rules, missing tools, or language standards
       │
       ▼
[Step 2: Update Governance Artifacts]
  ├── Update or author rules in .agents/rules/
  ├── Update or author skills in .agents/skills/
  └── Update or author workflows in .agents/workflows/
       │
       ▼
[Step 3: Synchronize AGENTS.md]
  └── Enshrine new invariants in the root master governance specification
```
